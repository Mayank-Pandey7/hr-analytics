2. Root Cause Diagnostic: Why Are Employees Leaving?

Empirical findings derived from statistical hypothesis tests and regression analysis

Primary Driver p < 0.0001

Overtime Multiplies Attrition by 3.4x

Staff assigned to regular overtime leave at a rate of 56.4%, compared to just 16.4% for standard-hour staff.

💡 Takeaway: Overtime is an operational burnout trigger, not an isolated preference.
Compensation Factor p < 0.0001

The $3,500 Salary Threshold

Employees earning under \$3,500/month experience 41.7% attrition. Turnover drops to 6.1% for salaries over \$7,000.

💡 Takeaway: Median leaver salary is \$2,515 vs \$3,375 for retained staff (\$860 gap).
Department Risk N = 326

HR & Sales Face Steepest Departures

Human Resources suffered 46.2% attrition, while Sales lost 31.3%. R&D had the lowest relative loss (25.4%).

💡 Takeaway: HR is the lowest-paid division (\$3,070 avg), creating severe talent retention friction.
💡
Critical Non-Finding: Departing staff held an average performance score of 3.15 out of 4.00 (identical to retained peers at 3.14), and 93.4% had received a promotion in the past 3 years. This confirms the organization is losing valuable, high-performing talent to workload and compensation mismatch, not offboarding low performers.

3. Strategic HR Recommendations & Roadmap

Four concrete policy interventions targeted at cutting attrition from 27.9% to <18%

Pillar 1 • Immediate (30 Days)

Cap Overtime Hours & Provide Comp-Off

Institute an 8-hour weekly overtime cap per worker in laboratory and sales divisions. Provide 1:1 compensatory rest days within 30 days of overtime to eliminate chronic fatigue.

Pillar 2 • Medium Term (60 Days)

Benchmark Entry-Level Salaries to $3,200+

Adjust starting base pay for Sales Representatives (avg \$2,580) and Lab Techs (avg \$2,955) to regional market median (\$3,200–\$3,500) to stop entry-tier compensation flight.

Pillar 3 • 60 Days

First-Year "Flight-Risk" Mentorship

46.2% of staff leave in Year 1. Establish 30-60-90 day milestone check-ins and pair new hires under 25 with mentors having 5+ years of tenure.

Pillar 4 • Immediate

HR Division Tooling & Parity Review

Automate repetitive HR administrative paperwork to reduce burnout, and bring HR specialist salaries into parity with cross-departmental peers.
